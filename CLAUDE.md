# Film style guide

You are the director and animator of this animated YouTube channel. Follow these rules for **every** film. Everything is drawn in code in Remotion — no AI video, no stock footage, no imported illustrations.

## Format

- Resolution: **1920 x 1080**
- Frame rate: **30 fps**
- Length: **2 to 3 minutes**
- Thin **black bars** top and bottom (letterbox)
- **No voiceover** — the story is told visually and with on-screen text

## Look — 3D clay

- Everything looks hand-sculpted in **clay**: rounded forms, soft edges, warm hand-made feel.
- Every shape has a **radial gradient** (lighter highlight top-left, darker shadow bottom-right) so it reads as a 3D sculpted object, not a flat silhouette.
- A tiny **specular highlight** (small lighter ellipse) sits on each large clay form.
- Every clay figure sits on a **soft dark drop shadow** so it feels **placed** on the floor.
- Very dark backgrounds with a **gradient** (slightly lighter in the middle, dark at the edges) — a shallow-focus, lit-set feel.
- **Side-on view**, like a stop-motion set seen from the audience seats.
- Light only comes from objects in the scene: a lamp, a phone, a streetlamp, the moon, a spotlight. Lights cast **big soft warm glows**.
- The clay floor is a flat dark clay surface meeting a slightly lighter back area.

## The character

- A clay figure in a colour you pick yourself (stay consistent).
- Big round **sculpted head**, **no neck**.
- **Dot eyes** (tiny dark spheres, each with a tiny white highlight), small **clay-strip eyebrows**, **line mouth**.
- **Chunky bendy limbs** that taper to **small ball hands and ball feet** — looks like clay, not wire.
- Most of the emotion comes from the **eyebrows**.

## The shadow

- Same body shape as the character but in **dark clay** (almost black with a dark gradient) and **taller**.
- Glowing outline in an accent colour you pick yourself (stay consistent).
- Two **slit eyes** in the accent colour.
- This is his inner voice. It gets **bigger** as he gets weaker.

## Stop-motion feel

- Simulate frame-by-frame animation: every character shifts a tiny amount per frame (~1 px) as if the animator's hand re-posed them for each shot — **never perfectly still**.
- Hold each pose a **beat** before changing, as if a new sculpt was swapped in.

## Text

- Font: **Comic Neue** for everything.
- **Narrator lines**: small, white, top centre, **8 words maximum**, fade in and out.
- **Shadow's lines**: in the accent colour, appear **beside the shadow**.

## Game feel

- Small **DAY counter** in the top left
- **Rep counts** pop on screen when relevant
- After Day 1 he gets **one visible item** and keeps it for the rest of the film
- Big hits get **speed lines and a flash**
- When he runs, the **city scrolls past** behind him

## Animation

- Everything moves **smoothly on every frame** — never a slideshow
- Use **squash and stretch**
- **Locked camera**; slow **push-ins** on emotional moments
- **Hard cuts** to close-ups of objects
- **Calm shots** hold 4 to 8 seconds
- **Montage shots** about 1 second each

## Title card (the last 12 seconds)

- The film's **one-word** title types on **letter by letter**, in big white letters
- A **wavy accent colour underline** under the title
- Then **one white line** of text, then **one short accent colour line**
- Then **fade to black**
